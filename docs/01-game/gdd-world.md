## Questions this file will answer

1. **What the Club is**: a group of retired people gather in one place — for what reason? Not "to save the world".
2. **Why the Delve exists**: why do they go down there? The permit mechanism in the economy needs an explanation from the world that holds up.
3. **The pasts of the four leads**: Lu Laosan, Su Jiuniang, the old Talisman-Maker, Zhong Bu'er. One core biography each, no longer than 200 characters.
4. **The period**: what kind of era made these people, and then discarded them?
5. **How the story is delivered**: this game uses **no cutscenes**. The story arrives through the Codex, inscriptions on gear, small talk from NPCs, and environmental storytelling inside the Delve.
6. **Lineage and Legacy**: what does what a character leaves behind after retiring mean in narrative terms?

## Tone discipline (non-negotiable, from `vision.md`)

**Do**: worn but upright. Tired but decent. Dry humour. Restrained regret for what is gone.

**Do not**:
- Beg for pity. These people are not pitiable. They are simply old.
- Play it adolescent. No last-stand declarations.
- Preach. Do not use a character's mouth to deliver lessons about life.
- Over-explain. What is left out carries more weight than what is filled in.

> One example, for calibration:
> Lu Laosan will never say "I was the best there was."
> He will, while showing a beginner how to hold a blade, correct the angle of the wrist
> before he has thought about it, then catch himself, and take his hand back.
